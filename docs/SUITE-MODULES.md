# 360° Business Suite

One company workspace with shared identity, organization membership, navigation, and audit history.

| Area | Scope | Current state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CRM and sales | Accounts, contacts, leads, pipeline, quotes, sales orders | Pilot CRM accounts and ERPNext customer links. Sales orders and invoices have read views. Pipeline and quote workflows pending. |
| Finance | Accounts, invoices, receipts and payments, journals, ledger, financial statements | Read views in the pilot. Posting and statements use ERPNext after connection. |
| Procurement and inventory | Suppliers, purchase orders, approvals, warehouses, stock movements, replenishment | Purchase order read view. Remaining workflows pending. |
| Projects and work | Projects, sections, tasks, assignments, collaboration, workload | Interactive preview and D1 schema. Live APIs pending. |
| Service desk | Customer tickets, assignment, SLAs, knowledge base | Planned. |
| HR and payroll | Staff records, leave, attendance, payroll approvals | Planned. Payroll needs country-specific rules. |
| Operations | Documents, approval workflows, reporting, dashboards, integrations | Planned. |
| Platform administration | Company onboarding, roles, subscriptions, billing and advertising | Staging membership and public ad fallback. Commercial workflows pending. |

## Work management requirements

Reference the [Asana feature directory](https://asana.com/features) and [plan comparison](https://asana.com/pricing) for capability coverage. The suite has its own Arabic-first interface and connects projects to company operations through role-scoped references.

| Area | Capabilities | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tasks and projects | List, Kanban board, calendar, sections, assignees, due dates, priorities, task duplication, filters and task detail | Available in the public preview. Preview edits are temporary. |
| Scheduling | Start dates, subtasks, milestones, dependencies, recurring work, timeline and Gantt | Planned. |
| Collaboration | Comments, mentions, collaborators, inbox, notifications, attachments, approvals, proofing and shared task membership across projects | Section collaborator controls are in the preview. Remaining features and live permissions are pending. |
| Workflow | Forms, templates, custom fields, reusable rules, intake routing, approvals and workflow automation | Planned. |
| Leadership | Portfolios, goals, progress updates, dashboards, workload, estimated and actual time, and resource capacity | Planned. |
| Enterprise | Access controls, guests, audit logs, lifecycle management, import/export, API, integrations, mobile experience and AI-assisted workflows | Pilot sign-in exists. Remaining work is scoped by feature and role. |
| Suite integrations | Link tasks to CRM customers, sales opportunities, quotes, orders, invoices, purchasing approvals, inventory and support tickets | CRM-to-ERP documents have a pilot integration. Work-management links are pending. |
