# 360° Business Suite

One company workspace with shared identity, organization membership, navigation, and audit history.

| Area | Scope | Current state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CRM and sales | Accounts, contacts, leads, pipeline, quotes, sales orders | Pilot CRM accounts and ERPNext customer links. Sales orders and invoices have read views. Pipeline and quote workflows pending. |
| Finance | Accounts, invoices, receipts and payments, journals, ledger, financial statements | Read views in the pilot. Posting and statements use ERPNext after connection. |
| Procurement and inventory | Suppliers, purchase orders, approvals, warehouses, stock movements, replenishment | Purchase order read view. Remaining workflows pending. |
| Projects and work | Projects, sections, tasks, assignments, collaboration, workload | Interactive preview and D1 schema. Live APIs pending. |
| Service desk | Customer tickets, assignment, SLAs, knowledge base | Planned. |
| HR and payroll | Staff records, leave, attendance, payroll approvals | Planned. Payroll needs country-specific rules. |
| Operations | Documents, approval workflows, reporting, dashboards, integrations | Planned. |
| Platform administration | Company onboarding, roles, subscriptions, billing and advertising | Staging membership and public ad fallback. Commercial workflows pending. |
